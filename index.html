Forecasts: Open-Meteo (CC BY 4.0) serving ECMWF, NOAA GFS, DWD ICON, UK Met Office, Météo-France and KNMI, plus ECMWF (51) and GEFS (31) ensembles. Live observations and tide predictions: Harwich Haven Authority (hha.co.uk), heights above Harwich chart datum; Open-Meteo marine model (MSL) as fallback. Tide size: predicted range against Harwich springs 3.6 m / neaps 2.3 m. Windy, XC Weather, BBC and PredictWind draw on these same models. Live wind comes from harbour-authority feeds where we have them (Harwich Haven at Levington) and from airport observation reports anywhere in the world, which are hourly and often daytime-only abroad. Tide gauges: Harwich Haven at Levington, the Environment Agency elsewhere in the UK. Forecasts, ensembles and the modelled tide work everywhere.
